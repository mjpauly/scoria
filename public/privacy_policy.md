# Privacy Policy

Privacy is why Epsilon exists. We believe there are enormous benefits to
thinking about information in terms of privacy. Our goal is to provide services
which not only exceed the industry standard in privacy and security, but also
shift how people think about private information itself.

## No Information Shared Unless Explicitly Consented

Epsilon is designed to help you collect and understand your own personal
information. Data collected by the app is private and only visible to you.

By using our services, you do not automatically consent to data collection.
Epsilon does not collect any logged data, usage statistics, or
personally-identifiable information, unless you explicitly share it with us.
"Explicit" sharing means that you are aware your information is being shared at
the time that it happens, and that you are aware what information is being
shared. Currently, this only occurs if you submit feedback to us, like when
reporting a bug.

## App Feedback

Information is only ever collected from you if you send us feedback, either
through an in-app feedback form, email, or other means. The contents of your
feedback submission will be visible to us, and will be used solely for improving
our services.

## Third-Party Maps

Epsilon uses a third-party maps provider to show you maps in the app. The
provider is Maptiler AG, and their privacy policy is available
[here](https://www.maptiler.com/privacy-policy/). We have selected Maptiler for
their privacy-forward and easy-to-understand privacy policy.

Map areas you view in Epsilon are loaded from Maptiler's servers. When you open
the map, your approximate viewing region is sent to Maptiler so the map data can
be downloaded. When you move to a new area of the map, the new map data is
requested from Maptiler if it hasn't already been downloaded in that session.
Maptiler states that they do not use this information for anything except to
provide you map data and prevent malicious activity on their servers. In
Epsilon, private information displayed over the map is never sent to Maptiler.

Aside from Maptiler, information is not sent to any other third parties.

Since map data is loaded over the internet, your internet service provider (ISP)
may see that you are connecting to Maptiler's servers. Map data loaded
from Maptiler is encrypted in transit, so your ISP cannot see what map regions
you are looking at.

## Summary

- Epsilon does not collect any information from you, unless you send us
feedback.
- To load and view map data over the internet, the approximate region you are
looking at is sent to our third-party maps provider, Maptiler AG.
