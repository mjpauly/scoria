# Privacy Policy

Privacy is why Epsilon exists. We, the Epsilon team, believe there are enormous
benefits to encouraging individuals and society at large to think about
information in terms of privacy. We aim to provide services which not only
exceed the industry standard in privacy and security, but also shift how people
think about private information itself.

## No Information Shared Unless Explicitly Consented

Epsilon is designed to help you collect and understand your own personal
information. Data collected by the app is private and only visible to you.

By using our services, you do not automatically consent to data collection.
Epsilon does not collect any logged data, usage statistics, or
personally-identifiable information, unless you explicitly share it with us.
"Explicit" sharing means that you are aware your information is being shared at
the time that it happens, and that you are aware what information is being
shared. Currently, this only occurs if you submit a bug report.

## Bug Reports

Information is only ever collected from you if you submit a bug report.
If submitted via email, we will see your name and email address. If the bug
report contains a screenshot from Epsilon, then the contents of the screenshot
will be visible to us. The information in any bug reports will be used solely
for resolving the reported bugs.

## Third Parties

Epsilon uses a third-party maps provider to show you maps in the app. The
provider is Maptiler AG, and their privacy policy is available
[here](https://www.maptiler.com/privacy-policy/). We have selected Maptiler for
their privacy-forward policies.

Map areas you view in Epsilon are loaded from Maptiler's servers. As a result,
the map areas you view and when you view them are sent to Maptiler. Maptiler
states that they do not use this information for anything except to provide you
map data and prevent malicious activity on their servers. In Epsilon, private
information displayed over the map is never sent to Maptiler.

Aside from Maptiler, information is not sent to any other third parties.

Since map data is loaded over the internet, your internet service provider (ISP)
may see that you are connecting to Maptiler's servers. Map data loaded
from Maptiler is encrypted while passing through the internet, so your ISP
cannot see what map regions you are looking at.

## Summary

- Epsilon does not collect any information from you, unless you submit a bug
report.
- To load and view map data over the internet, the region you are looking at is
sent to our third-party maps provider, Maptiler AG.
