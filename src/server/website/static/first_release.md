# Epsilon's First Release

*Jul 12, 2023*

We're delighted to share that
[Epsilon: Private Location Log](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6448646570) is now
available for iPhone on the App Store. (Android users, sit tight!) If you're
excited by the idea of creating beautiful and detailed maps of your location
history, all with your data staying on your device, then consider giving Epsilon
a try.

<img src="/screenshots/1.jpeg" class="w-[256px] rounded-[32px] border-2 border-neutral-700 my-8 mx-auto">

While location capabilities have been around since the first smartphones,
their power efficiency has improved tremendously in the past few years.
This makes it easier than ever to use location services continuously, opening
the door for new ways to explore your own activity, travel, and the places you
visit. That said, depending on your phone model and movement, Epsilon can still
use considerable power. But with a range of low power modes available, you can
easily tune the balance between data accuracy and battery life to fit your
needs.

If you're trying Epsilon for the first time, we recommend taking it to
explore your neighborhood. Try going down new streets, since this will make the
path Epsilon logs more interesting to compare with your memory. And as you
collect more data, Epsilon shows you what places are still new and unexplored.

## Motivated by Privacy

Location services are integral to many modern apps. We are frequently asked to
make decisions about whether we want to share location information about
ourselves, and with who. This can be challenging, especially since we usually
don't have a clear picture of what our location data actually looks like. We
might remember where we went in the past week, but activity from months ago can
be harder to recall. How much does your location data reveal about you anyways?

We believe that understanding your own personal data is a valuable ingredient in
making informed decisions about your privacy. With Epsilon, we hope to make this
discovery easy, accessible, and enjoyable.

We have many more features planned for Epsilon. However, we won't spoil them
just yet. Some of these features will be paid, and will comprise our business
model. Though Epsilon is early in development, we want to emphasize that all
features released for free are intended to remain free and private without
compromise. We strive for a high standard of privacy, based on informed and
[explicit consent](/privacy) if any data is ever shared with anyone, including
us. Privacy is a big part of Epsilon's mission.

We hope this makes Epsilon a compelling choice for you, and we look forward to
hearing about your experiences with it!
